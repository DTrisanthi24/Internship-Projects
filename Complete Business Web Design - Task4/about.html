About Deccan Dhaba

At Deccan Dhaba, we believe food is more than just a meal — it’s an experience of tradition, taste, and togetherness. Located in the heart of Kukatpally, Hyderabad, near Metro Pillar 789, our dhaba brings the charm of rustic Indian dining into a modern, welcoming space. We specialize in serving authentic North and South Indian delicacies, prepared with fresh ingredients and a touch of home‑style cooking. From our signature Chicken Curry and Paneer Butter Masala to freshly made naans, pulkas, and biryanis, every dish is crafted to deliver comfort and flavor. Our mission is simple: To offer delicious food at affordable prices. To create a friendly atmosphere where families, friends, and food lovers can gather. To preserve the essence of traditional dhaba culture while embracing modern hospitality. Whether you’re stopping by for a quick bite or planning a hearty dinner, Deccan Dhaba promises a memorable dining experience that blends taste, tradition, and warmth.
